Adidas Sales Report

Summary:
Developed a business-focused Power BI dashboard to analyze Adidas sales performance across products, regions, and retailers. The report provides stakeholders with actionable insights into sales dynamics, profitability, and market potential, highlighting high-performing products, regions, and retailers. It enables data-driven decisions for inventory management, marketing optimization, pricing strategies, and margin improvement, helping drive revenue growth and operational efficiency.

Technical Focus:
• Cleaned and transformed raw sales data using Power Query, ensuring data accuracy and consistency for critical fields such as date, price, and units sold
• Built DAX measures for total sales, profit, margin percentages, average price per unit, and units sold to track key KPIs
• Designed interactive visuals including line/area charts, geographical maps, and bar charts for trends, product, retailer, and regional performance
• Implemented dynamic slicers and filters for easy exploration by time period, product, region, and retailer
• Focused on delivering business-ready insights that support revenue growth, strategic pricing, marketing effectiveness, and inventory planning

Car Sales Analysis

Summary:
Created a Power BI car sales performance dashboard to track YTD and MTD sales, average selling price, and weekly revenue trends. Equipped business leaders to compare results by body style, color, dealer region, and manufacturer, helping them spot high growth segments and adjust pricing and inventory strategies.

Technical Focus:
• Designed a semantic model with DAX measures for YTD/MTH sales, YTD average price, and percentage growth.
• Built interactive visuals including a weekly sales area chart, donut charts for sales by body style and color, a map for YOY growth by dealer region, and a company wise performance table with conditional formatting.
• Enabled cross filtering and drill through so selections on visuals automatically update KPIs and detailed views.
• Applied a clean, executive friendly layout with consistent theming, K notation for large numbers, and sortable tabular views to support quick decision making.

Amazon Sales Dashboard

Summary:
An interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze Amazon sales performance with a focus on business insights and decision support. The report provides clear visibility into year-to-date and quarter-to-date sales, product demand, and customer engagement trends. By highlighting seasonal patterns, top-performing products, and high-impact categories, the dashboard enables stakeholders to make informed decisions related to sales strategy, inventory planning, and customer experience optimization.

Technical Focus:
• Cleaned and transformed raw sales data using Power Query, ensuring data quality by validating critical fields such as date, price, and null values.
• Created DAX measures for YTD and QTD sales, products sold, and customer reviews.
• Built time-based analysis using monthly and weekly aggregations to capture both long-term trends and short-term fluctuations.
• Designed interactive visuals including line charts, bar charts, and heatmaps for category-level and product-level insights
• Implemented slicers and filters to allow users to dynamically explore sales performance across time periods and product categories.
• Followed best practices in dashboard design, focusing on business KPIs and usability for non-technical stakeholders.

E-Commerce Sales Dashboard

Summary:
Designed a Power BI ecommerce sales dashboard to track total sales, profit, and quantity across time, product categories, and cities. Provided management with quick insights into best performing categories and sub categories, plus monthly profit trends, to support pricing and inventory decisions.

Technical Focus:
• Built star schema model with a sales fact table (Amount, Profit, Quantity) and dimension tables for Date, Category, Sub Category, and City, using DAX measures for KPIs ( Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Quantity).
• Implemented interactive visuals (cards, donut chart, bar charts, monthly trend chart) with drill through and cross filtering for detailed performance analysis by product and time.
• Used slicers for Quarter and City to enable dynamic, page level filtering and scenario analysis for regional and seasonal performance.
• Applied consistent theming, custom background, and formatted numeric display (K notation, labels, tooltips) to enhance readability and executive level presentation.
